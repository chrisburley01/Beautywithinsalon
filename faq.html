Frequently Asked Questions

What can help the appearance of fine lines?

Depending on your skin and goals, facial massage, facials, skin-care routines and other non-prescription treatments may help improve the skin's appearance. These are separate services with different expected outcomes. Joanne can discuss options and prices at a consultation.

How long do dermal fillers last?

Results vary depending on the product, treatment area and individual circumstances. Joanne can discuss expected duration, aftercare and risks at consultation.

What are skin boosters and who are they for?

Skin boosters are cosmetic treatments intended to support aspects of skin appearance. Suitability, ingredients, the treatment plan and potential risks need to be assessed individually.

Do you treat men?

Yes, other aesthetic and skin-care treatments may be available to people of all genders. The scalp micropigmentation service advertised here is specifically for women with female pattern hair loss.

What is ladies' scalp micropigmentation (SMP)?

Scalp micropigmentation uses cosmetic pigments to give thinning scalp areas the appearance of greater hair density. It is an appearance treatment, not a medical treatment for hair loss, and it does not regrow hair.

Who can book SMP at Beautywithin?

Our SMP service is for women experiencing female pattern hair loss (female pattern baldness) only, subject to consultation and suitability. We do not advertise SMP for male pattern hair loss or other hair-loss conditions. Unexplained or sudden hair loss should be assessed by a healthcare professional.

How much does women's SMP cost?

The cost depends on the scalp area and agreed treatment plan. Joanne will provide a personalised quotation following a consultation. No fixed SMP price is advertised.
