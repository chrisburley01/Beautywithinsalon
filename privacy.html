Privacy Policy

Beautywithin Aesthetics respects your privacy. This notice explains how we handle information when you visit our website, contact Joanne or enquire about appointments and treatments. Last reviewed: 23 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Beautywithin Aesthetics, operated by Joanne Burley, is responsible for personal information used in connection with the business. Our contact address is 67 Woodkirk Gardens, Woodkirk, WF12 7JA, United Kingdom. To ask about your information, call or message 07714 201913.

2. What information might we handle?

  • Contact and enquiry details such as your name, telephone number, preferred appointment time and information you choose to send by phone or WhatsApp.
  • Consultation and appointment details needed to assess, arrange or provide services.
  • Where relevant to a consultation or treatment, information about health, allergies, medications and treatment history, plus photographs, only where appropriately collected and required.
  • Basic technical information that our hosting, map and font providers may receive when you use the website or choose to load an external service.

You do not need to share sensitive medical details in an initial WhatsApp enquiry. Please discuss any necessary health information through the consultation process.

3. Why do we use information?

We use the minimum information reasonably needed to answer enquiries, arrange consultations, manage appointments, provide services and meet legal or professional obligations. Depending on the activity, the UK GDPR lawful basis may be our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, taking steps at your request before a contract, performing a contract, or complying with a legal obligation. We do not use health information for advertising.

Health information is special-category data and requires an additional lawful condition under Article 9 UK GDPR. Any relevant consent, treatment-record information and retention arrangements must be explained and documented as part of the consultation and client-record process. Separate consent is sought before client photographs are used for promotional purposes.

4. Who may receive your information?

Information may be handled by service providers necessary for communications, appointment administration, secure storage, website hosting or professional advice, with access limited to what is necessary. If you contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp/Meta also handles data under its own privacy terms. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and Google provides the optional map and website fonts. We do not sell personal information.

5. Cookies and third-party website content

This website does not currently run its own marketing or analytics tracking script. Google Fonts is used for typography. When the embedded location map loads or you open an external link, that provider may receive technical information and use cookies under its own policies. You can read Google's privacy policy, WhatsApp's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

6. How long is information kept?

Enquiry and appointment information is kept only for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry, manage the client relationship and meet any applicable legal, insurance or professional requirements. Treatment records, if held, are retained according to the applicable documented record-retention policy. Information is securely deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed.

7. Security and transfers

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information and restrict access to those who need it. Some external services may process data outside the UK; where relevant, the service provider is responsible for applying appropriate international data-transfer safeguards.

8. Your privacy rights

Subject to legal conditions and exceptions, you may ask to access, correct or erase information, restrict or object to some uses, or request portability where applicable. If we rely on consent, you can withdraw it for future use. Contact Joanne using the number above to make a request.

If you are dissatisfied with how we use your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services, suppliers or legal obligations change. The review date at the top indicates when this page was last updated.
