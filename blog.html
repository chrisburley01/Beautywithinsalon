Comfortable Skin Through the Colder Months

Cool weather and indoor heating can leave skin feeling dry. A consistent, gentle routine is often more helpful than chasing quick fixes.

  • Hydration: Choose a moisturiser suited to your skin.
  • Sun protection: Consider appropriate SPF throughout the year.
  • Facial care: Ask Joanne about suitable facial massage and non-prescription skin-care options.

Skin Boosters: Questions to Ask Before Treatment

Different cosmetic treatments are marketed as skin boosters. Before you book, ask about the product, suitability, contraindications, aftercare, possible risks and realistic outcomes.

  • What product is being considered, and why?
  • How many appointments might be needed?
  • Are there suitable non-injectable alternatives?

Women’s SMP for Female Pattern Hair Loss: What to Know

Scalp micropigmentation (SMP) places tiny cosmetic pigment impressions on the scalp to create the appearance of greater hair density in thinning areas.

Beautywithin offers SMP only to women with female pattern hair loss (female pattern baldness). SMP is a cosmetic appearance treatment: it does not regrow hair or stop the underlying cause of thinning. Results and maintenance needs vary.

A consultation covers suitability, expected appearance, healing, aftercare and pricing. If your hair loss is unexplained, seek medical assessment before cosmetic treatment.

Aftercare Tips: Caring for Your Skin

Aftercare depends on the treatment. Follow the personalised instructions Joanne provides, rather than assuming the same routine works for every facial, peel, injectable or SMP service.

  • Contact the practitioner if you have concerns about your recovery.
